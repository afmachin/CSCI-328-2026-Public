GraduateStudent evaluation

Two Liskov violations in GraduateStudent.enroll:

1. Stronger precondition: Student.enroll accepts a non-null offering without
requiring advisor approval. GraduateStudent.enroll requires approval first,
so the subtype accepts fewer calls than the parent method.

2. Weaker postcondition and broader failure behavior: Student.enroll adds
the offering to the student's enrollments and increases its enrolled count.
Without approval, the override throws IllegalStateException and does neither.
A caller using a Student reference can no longer rely on the parent's
normal result for that call.

Advisor approval is a real registration rule. It belongs in
RegistrationService.register, alongside the other eligibility checks, just
before the service calls student.enroll. The service can then return false
and explain the rejection through lastErrors. Once these rules are in the
registration flow, they could be moved into a separate enrollment policy
instead of adding more enrollment overrides to student subclasses.

Changes made: I removed the enroll override from GraduateStudent but kept
its approval field and accessors. I added an approval check in
RegistrationService before enrollment changes occur. Approved graduate
students still use Student.enroll. A full section can still put a graduate
student on the waitlist, as it could before this change.

Improvement cost: RegistrationService now checks for GraduateStudent
specifically. More student types could make that service harder to maintain
unless the rules move into a policy. Also, callers can still bypass the
service by calling the public Student.enroll method or changing its exposed
enrollment list. Preventing that would require restricting those mutation
paths and updating their callers, which is a larger refactor.
